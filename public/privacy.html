Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EnsoAI Team ("EnsoAI", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the EnsoAI desktop application, our website, and any related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account & billing data — when you purchase a paid plan, our payment processor (Creem) collects your name, email address, billing country, and payment method information. We receive a transaction record but do not store your full card details.
  • Support communications — when you contact us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • License & activation data — a device identifier and your license key are exchanged with our servers to validate your subscription or lifetime purchase.
  • Diagnostic data — anonymized crash logs and aggregated usage counters (e.g., feature opens, error codes) used solely to improve product quality. You can disable this in the application settings.
  • Website analytics — basic page-view metrics (referrer, user-agent, country) on ensoai marketing pages.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

EnsoAI processes your source code, repository contents, terminal output, and AI conversations locally on your device. We do not transmit, read, or store the contents of your projects on our servers. When you connect a third-party AI provider (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, etc.), prompts and responses are sent directly from your device to that provider under that provider's own privacy terms.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and operate the Service;
  • Validate licenses and process payments;
  • Respond to support requests and product feedback;
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or abuse;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share limited data only with:

  • Payment processor — Creem (creem.io) processes payments and handles billing communications.
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting and email delivery vendors that operate under standard data-processing agreements.
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain billing records for the period required by tax and accounting law (typically 7 years). Support emails are retained for up to 24 months. Diagnostic telemetry is retained in aggregated form for up to 12 months.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws), you may request to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, email admin@j3.do. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. We rely on standard contractual safeguards where required.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS, encrypted storage, access controls). No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password and keep your devices updated.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
